     and S = (D11+D22+2 D12)/4 = 48.32 N mm from the same fit.)

    THE TWO IDENTIFIED ROUTES AGREE TO 6.7%: 9.87 (45 deg, indirect)
    against 10.35 (twist, direct), and 10.56 with both stacked.

    That is the result worth having.  The two routes share almost no
    assumptions: the 45 deg answer comes from a ratio of curvatures
    in which the moment cancels, so no specimen width, machine
    calibration or moment gradient enters it, but it does depend on
    the 45/135 cut sense and on a twist the yoke suppresses ~15x.
    The twist answer comes from a directly loaded kappa_xy, is
    indifferent to the cut sense, and instead scales as 1/width and
    depends on its own unrecorded cut angle (a factor of 2.7 --
    see ../twist_three_coupon section 6).  Two estimators with
    disjoint dominant errors landing within a few percent of each
    other is evidence neither is dominated by its own weakness.

    THE CLOSED FORM IS THE OUTLIER at 21.64 N mm, and that is
    a statement about S, not about D66.  D66 = S(1+r)/(1-r) is
    linear in S, and S = 48.32 N mm here carries a D12 of
    -7.94 -- negative, i.e. the campaign's weakest
    parameter.  The closed form inherits that weakness undiluted;
    the stacked fits do not.
